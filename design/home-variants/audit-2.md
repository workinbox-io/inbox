## 3. The five changes that would sell the product best

1. **Replace every screenshot with the faux-UI set, and make it the proof of the "everything links to everything" claim.** On home, one hero faux that shows a mail thread with an @mention chip, a Drive attachment and the Cmd-K palette in the same frame; on app pages, each app's faux with the link to another app visible (calendar event with its meeting note, chat message with a mail-thread chip). This is the single claim no alternative page can make and today it is only in words.
2. **Fix the hero contrast and alignment on sub-pages and cut the density** (N3, N5): h1, one lead sentence, one CTA row, price as a small line on a dark ground. The first screen of every app page is what search traffic lands on.
3. **Finish the Google purge from the home and footer** (N4): drop "Replaces X", rename the "Switch" footer column, keep Google only in "Getting started" origin logos, /vs, /pricing and the guide. The rule of thumb in copy.md is still violated in nine places.
4. **Add one customer-shaped proof block to the home** between Shared inbox and AI: a single "a day in Acme Studio" strip (the demo's story: Marta's contract, Anna's draft, Ben's take-over, the meeting note, the Drive file) with a "Try this exact workspace" button into the demo. The demo is the strongest conversion lever and it is only reachable via buttons; show what is in it.
5. **Polish the Polish and the language fallbacks** (N7, N8): translate the faux content on /pl, unify "szkic/wersja robocza", and either translate /custom-apps and /cloudflare-email-routing or stop labelling them "EN". Native readers decide trust in the first screen, and the Polish market is the one with a localized site.

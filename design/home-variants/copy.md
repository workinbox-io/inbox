# Work Inbox copy: product first

Rule of thumb: every headline must be true and interesting with the word "Google" deleted. Comparisons live in /vs, /google-workspace-alternative, one price line lower on the home page, and some meta titles.
`*word*` = the `<em>` key word. Polish sits under each English line. Only shipped facts (checked against CLAUDE.md).

## 1. Positioning and pillars

**One sentence.** Work Inbox is the team workspace where mail, chat, notes, calendar, files and contacts are one connected thing, on your own domain, open source.
**PL:** Work Inbox to przestrzeń pracy zespołu, w której poczta, czat, notatki, kalendarz, pliki i kontakty są jedną całością: na Twojej domenie, z otwartym kodem.

Pillars, ranked:

1. **Everything links to everything.** Strongest product claim and the one nobody can say in one sentence of "alternative" copy. Real, demoable: `@` anything (people, files, notes, events, contacts, mail threads) in chat and mail, one ⌘K, attach from Drive / save to Drive, one notification bell, a meeting note from an event, a Collection record that opens from a chat mention. Why #1: it is what working in it feels like, and it is the reason to use one workspace instead of six tabs.
   PL: Wszystko łączy się ze wszystkim. (`@` do czegokolwiek, jedno ⌘K, jeden dzwonek powiadomień.)
2. **Built for teams that share mail.** Shared mailboxes (support@) that feel right: "Draft by Anna" read-only so nobody answers twice, take over, signatures per mailbox, out of office, templates, per-person drafts that sync to Apple Mail. Why #2: the most distinctive mail behaviour, concrete, visual (the 4-step story already exists).
   PL: Stworzone dla zespołów ze wspólną skrzynką.
3. **Yours, and fairly priced.** Open source (AGPL-3.0), runs on your own Cloudflare account with every feature, data leaves readable (.eml, vCard, CSV, .ics, ZIP, IMAP); hosted is free for 5 people, then EUR 1 per person a month, shared mailboxes free. Why #3: a trust and risk-removal pillar, not the reason to want it; it closes the deal after pillars 1-2 sold the product.
   PL: Twoje i w uczciwej cenie.

Supporting (not a pillar, weave in): AI apps (Claude, ChatGPT via MCP; mail is drafted, never sent on the model's word), Collections (your own lists like Linear), the no-signup demo.

## 6. Voice and words

- Voice: plain, specific, second person, present tense, verbs about work ("mention", "take over", "attach"). Numbers when we have them, adjectives never. Short sentences.
- Show a moment, not a feature list: "Ben sees 'Draft by Anna'" beats "collaborative drafts".
- Avoid: "alternative to" in headlines, "without the Google bill", "replaces X" as a banner, "leave Google", "switch" as the main verb (use "start", "bring", "connect"), "revolutionary", "all-in-one" / "seamless" / "supercharge", "cheap", "powerful", testimonials or user counts we cannot show.
- OK, sparingly: Google/Gmail/Slack as origin labels in import rows and /vs pages, and one honest "Good to know" box on gaps.
- Polish: "Twoja/Twoje" capitalised as elsewhere, "przestrzeń pracy" for workspace, "wersja robocza" for draft, "skrzynka" not "skrzynka pocztowa" when short, no calques like "all-in-one" -> "wszystko w jednym". Keep the brand "Work Inbox".
- Claim only what ships: no CalDAV/CardDAV, booking pages, native mobile apps, voice/video calls.

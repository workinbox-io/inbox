Questions before you switch

What we don't do

  • No native phone apps (installable web app and IMAP/SMTP instead)
  • No CalDAV or CardDAV, no booking pages
  • No Docs, Sheets or Meet; no voice or video calls
Who to pick instead, case by case

Still unsure? Ask us at support@workinbox.io, a person answers. Or try the demo and look yourself.

What will I miss compared to Google Workspace?

There are no native phone apps (the web app installs on phones and computers, and IMAP and SMTP make Apple Mail, Outlook and phone mail apps work), no CalDAV or CardDAV, no booking pages, and no Docs, Sheets or Meet equivalents beyond shared notes and Collections. There are no voice or video calls. The comparison pages list what to pick them for: /vs.

Will my mail be delivered?

Hosted custom domains send through Amazon SES, and setup checks your DKIM, SPF and DMARC records before you switch. New unpaid workspaces have a sending cap for the first 14 days, and sending pauses automatically on bounces or complaints. Nobody can promise inbox placement; we make sure the technical side is right.

Do I have to move my domain or mailboxes first?

No. Connect the mailbox you already have over IMAP and SMTP and read and send from Work Inbox while mail keeps arriving where it does today. Copy the old mail in later, or upload a Takeout .mbox, and point the domain's MX here last. If your domain is on Cloudflare, Connect Cloudflare adds the records for you.

Do I need my own domain?

No. Claim a free address like yourname.workinbox.io in Settings, Mail and start right away. Add your own domain whenever you are ready.

Is Work Inbox really open source?

Yes. The code is AGPL-3.0, public at github.com/workinbox-io/inbox. You can run it on your own Cloudflare account with every feature and no fee to us; the hosted version is the same app, run by us.

What does a shared mailbox cost?

Nothing. You pay per person with a personal mailbox, from EUR 1 a month on Starter, and the first 5 people are free. Shared mailboxes, aliases and the catch-all are free on every plan.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every hosted account starts with a 14-day trial, no card needed, and up to 5 people stay free after it. Self-hosting is free for good.

Can I use Outlook, Apple Mail or my phone's mail app?

Yes. Every mailbox works over IMAP and SMTP: create a password for the device in Settings, Mail clients, and add the account in Apple Mail, Outlook, Thunderbird or your phone's mail app. "Connect device" can also hand you an Apple profile.

Can it replace Slack and Notion too?

For everyday team chat and shared notes, yes: channels, DMs, threads, mentions and files; notes with live editing, checklists, tables and mentions. For Notion-style databases there are Collections, lists with your own fields in table, board and timeline views.

Where is my data stored?

On Cloudflare: messages in D1, each original message (.eml) and its attachments in R2. Self-hosted, all of it lives on your own Cloudflare account. You can export it any time: .eml and IMAP, vCard or CSV, .ics, ZIP.

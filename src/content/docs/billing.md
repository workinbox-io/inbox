You pay per person: everyone with a personal mailbox on your domains. **The first person is free**, on every plan
and without one, so a team of five pays for four. Shared mailboxes and aliases are always free.

On your own, mail keeps working after the trial with no plan. From the second person, sending mail and adding
people, mailboxes or domains need a plan; incoming mail and forwarding rules always keep working. A plan for one person
(for the API, the AI connector or the audit log) is billed for one.

Payments are handled by Paddle, our merchant of record: they charge VAT where it applies and send
the invoices. Change your plan, payment method or cancel in **Billing → Invoices & payment**.

You pay per person: everyone with a personal mailbox on your domains. **The first five people are free**, on every
plan and without one. Shared mailboxes and aliases are always free.

Up to five people, mail keeps working after the trial with no plan. From the sixth person, sending mail and adding
people, mailboxes or domains need a plan; incoming mail and forwarding rules always keep working.

A plan bills the people beyond the first five, and at least five of them: Starter at €1 a person is €5 a month for up
to ten people, then €1 for each person more. A smaller team that buys a plan for the API, the AI connector or the
audit log pays that minimum too.

Payments are handled by Paddle, our merchant of record: they charge VAT where it applies and send
the invoices. Change your plan, payment method or cancel in **Billing → Invoices & payment**.

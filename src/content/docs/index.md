WorkInbox is open-source team email on your own domain, with shared notes. It runs on Cloudflare: hosted by us, or
self-hosted on your own Cloudflare account.

## What ships today

- **Mail**: a personal mailbox per person, shared mailboxes like support@, aliases, a catch-all per domain, plus
  addressing, a spam score and sender rules, search operators and keyboard shortcuts.
- **Notes**: notes your team edits together, live, shared with chosen people or the whole team.
- **AI apps and API**: read and answer mail and notes from Claude or ChatGPT ([AI apps](/docs/mcp)), or over the
  [REST API](/docs/api).

Calendar, contacts and drive are next. There is no IMAP access yet: mail is read and sent in the web app, which you can
install on a phone or a computer.

## Where to start

- [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart): from sign-up to your first mail.
- [Self-hosting](/docs/self-host): run WorkInbox on your own Cloudflare account.
- [Team and roles](/docs/team): who can see which mailboxes.

## 1. Create an account

[Sign up](/signup) with your email or Google. Every account starts with a free trial, no card needed.

## 2. Create a project

Open **Projects** in the dashboard and click **New project**.

## 3. Invite your team

Go to **Settings → Organization → Team** and invite people by email.
